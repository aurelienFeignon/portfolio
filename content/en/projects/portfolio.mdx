This site is built in phases, each closed by exit criteria verified by an execution — never
by a reading. Structuring decisions are written down in versioned ADRs, and each phase leaves a log of
what execution overturned.

Three constraints govern it, in this order: **accessibility, indexability, content performance**. The
3D layer that will come later is an enhancement, never a prerequisite — deleting its folder entirely
must leave a complete, navigable, indexable portfolio behind.

A few concrete consequences of that discipline:

- no Node tooling is installed on my machine: everything runs in containers;
- invalid content **breaks the build** rather than producing a half-empty page;
- no route can be rendered on demand, and a gate verifies it on every build;
- the site currently ships **no page-specific JavaScript at all**.

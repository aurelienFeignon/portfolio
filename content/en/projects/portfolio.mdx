This portfolio combines a **static, bilingual documentary foundation** with a motherboard
3D scene generated from its content. Skills become chips, projects become processors and
experiences form a chronological bus; traces represent their relationships.

## My work

- Design the Next.js and React architecture, MDX content schemas and bilingual routes.
- Generate a deterministic board plan from content and render it with Three.js and React Three Fiber.
- Keep content accessible without WebGL and load the scene as a progressive enhancement.
- Document decisions in ADRs and check content, static rendering and 3D isolation during builds.
- Automate tests, Docker image publication and deployment with GitHub Actions.

## Measuring on real hardware

The embedded diagnostic panel reports initial rendering and long tasks on computers and phones.
Software-rendered benchmark results remain separate from real-device measurements.
Content is being expanded; a visual revision is planned once it is complete.

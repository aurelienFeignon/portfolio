This exploration starts from **HazyResearch's Next-Concept Modeling**: a text generation
approach that predicts a hierarchy of representations from coarse to fine, generating positions
in parallel at each level.

## My exploration work

My local work focuses on experiment preparation: evaluation protocols, corpus manifests,
data splits and overlap prevention. I have also prepared content validators, a Qwen3 conversation
template, a generation client and an experiment registry to record execution conditions.

The goal is to make comparisons interpretable: which data is used, which protocol is fixed
and which conclusions the results actually support.

## Scope

**I did not create NCM.** The linked repository is my fork of HazyResearch's work; part of my
exploration remains local and may not be present in that fork. Preparing experiments does not
establish completed training or a demonstrated quality or speed improvement.

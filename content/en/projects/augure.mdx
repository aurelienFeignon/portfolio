Augure follows markets in real time and turns them into predictions: data flows in continuously,
models trained on those series produce their estimates, and every strategy built on them can be
tested against history before it is switched on.

What a user actually does with it:

- **build a model** — pick the series, the indicators and the horizon, then run the training and
  read its metrics;
- **compose a strategy** on top of their models' predictions, and **backtest** it on a test window,
  then on a recent sliding window;
- **ask the assistant**: it reads and acts within the platform, but never beyond what the user is
  allowed to see or do.

The project is also a **research laboratory**: hypotheses are written down before they are tested,
and a result only counts if the protocol that produced it is on record.

Dedale is a personal patent exploration project connecting an ingestion pipeline,
a hybrid search engine and corpus-grounded answer generation. The aim is to retrieve relevant
information while keeping its provenance accessible.

## My work

- Design TypeScript and Python services, ingestion workers and document storage.
- Combine lexical search and embeddings, with an explicit lexical fallback when vector search
  is unavailable.
- Identify embeddings by model, revision and configuration to prevent incompatible vectors
  from being mixed and support reindexing.
- Integrate CPU-based BGE-M3 and an independent, locally hosted OpenAI-compatible LLM server.
- Require structured outputs and corpus references rather than unrestricted, unsourced prose.

## Validation and limits

A local pipeline with synthetic documents supports checks of ingestion, indexing, retrieval
and generation. Simulated backends are distinguished from real models. The project also includes
semantic qualification procedures, retrieval and RAG evaluation, and local XML import with provenance.
**This does not validate coverage of worldwide patents**, nor prove that every generated claim
is supported by its citation. Source code is kept in a private repository.

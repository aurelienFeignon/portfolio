Askor helps companies measure the environmental impact of their products across their whole life
cycle — materials, manufacturing, transport, use, end of life — and find where to reduce it.

What a user actually does with it:

- **model their product** in a visual flow editor, step by step;
- **run the assessment** and follow its progress live, without reloading the page;
- **compare eco-design scenarios**: a module guides the choices through a weighted decision tree,
  and shows what each option changes in the result.

A result always stays tied to the emission factors that produced it: two assessments run months
apart remain comparable.
